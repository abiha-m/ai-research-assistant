Artificial intelligence (AI) is transforming the healthcare industry. Machine learning algorithms can now detect diseases like cancer and Alzheimer's from medical scans years before symptoms appear. AI is also helping doctors create personalized treatment plans based on genetic data and medical history. Pharmaceutical companies are using AI to speed up drug discovery and reduce clinical trial costs. While there are challenges like data privacy and algorithmic bias, AI is expected to make healthcare more accessible and effective in the coming years.
