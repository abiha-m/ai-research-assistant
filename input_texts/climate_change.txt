Climate change is one of the most pressing challenges of our time. Global temperatures are rising due to increased greenhouse gas emissions from human activities like burning fossil fuels and deforestation. The consequences include more frequent extreme weather events, rising sea levels, and loss of biodiversity. Scientists warn that without significant action, the impacts will worsen dramatically by 2050. However, renewable energy technologies like solar and wind power are becoming cheaper and more efficient. Electric vehicles are gaining popularity, and countries are setting ambitious net-zero targets. Individual actions like reducing waste, using public transport, and eating less meat can also make a difference. The transition to a sustainable future requires collective effort from governments, businesses, and individuals.
